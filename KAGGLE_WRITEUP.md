## 2. Project Description (max 250 words)

**AssignFlow** is a comprehensive mobile study companion designed to combat academic overwhelm. It unifies task management, focus tools, and AI-powered learning into a single, cohesive experience.

**How we created it:**
We built a full-stack cross-platform app (React/Capacitor + Node.js) centered around **Google Gemini 2.0 Flash**. Leveraging Gemini's multimodal capabilities, we built three core features:
1.  **Visual Solver:** Users snap photos of math problems; Gemini Vision analyzes the handwriting and outputs step-by-step logic.
2.  **AI Quiz Generator:** The app scans static notes and instantly generates interactive quizzes, automating active recall.
3.  **Smart Digitization:** It transforms messy handwritten pages into structured, digital summaries.

**Why it's important:**
Students often struggle to bridge the gap between "passive reading" and "active understanding." AssignFlow automates this bridge. By turning static notes into interactive quizzes and providing instant help on stuck problems, we democratize access to personalized tutoring. We are impacting students by reducing study anxiety and promoting evidence-based learning techniques through a simple, accessible interface.
