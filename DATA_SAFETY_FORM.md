# Data Safety Form Responses for Google Play Console

Use these responses when filling out the Data Safety form in Google Play Console.

---

## Overview Questions

### Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
**Yes**

### Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
**Yes** - All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS

### Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?
**Yes** - Users can delete their account from Settings → Profile → Delete Account

---

## Security Practices

### Data encrypted in transit
**Yes** - TLS 1.2+

### Data encrypted at rest
**Yes** - AES-256 encryption on database

### Users can request data deletion
**Yes** - Via Settings → Delete Account

### Data deletion policy
User data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.

---

## Third-Party Data Sharing

### Stripe (Payment Processing)
- **Data shared:** Email, payment info (via Stripe SDK)
- **Purpose:** Process Pro subscription payments
- **PCI Compliant:** Yes

### OpenAI / Google AI
- **Data shared:** User queries to AI assistant (anonymized)
- **Purpose:** AI study assistance features
- **No persistent storage** of user content

---

## Notes for Review

1. **OTA Updates:** App uses Capgo for web asset updates only, not for bypassing Play Store policies. Native code updates still require Play Store submission.

2. **AI Features:** AI responses are generated by OpenAI/Google and may not always be accurate. Users are advised this is for educational assistance only.

3. **User Data Control:** Users have full control of their data with export and delete options in Settings.

4. **Age Restriction:** App is designed for users 13+ (students). No features specifically target children.
